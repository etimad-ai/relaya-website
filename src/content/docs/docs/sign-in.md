title: Sign in and your workspace
description: How to sign in to Relaya, what a workspace is, and how new members join.
---

Relaya is a hosted product — there's nothing to install. You sign in through your identity provider (such as Google or Microsoft), and Relaya never sees or stores a password.

## Signing in

On the sign-in screen, continue with your work email through your provider. Relaya verifies your identity with them and brings you straight into your workspace.

:::note
Relaya is in a private pilot, so access is by invitation. If you were invited, sign in with the **same email address your invitation was sent to**.
:::

## What a workspace is

A **workspace** is your team's shared home in Relaya — the accounts, contacts, deals, connected mailboxes, and memory all live inside it. Everyone you invite works in the same workspace and sees the same customer picture.

## Creating a workspace

The first time an owner signs in, they name their workspace and become its first member. Creating a workspace requires a **work email** — a business address rather than a personal mailbox — because the workspace is a shared team account, and your email domain anchors its identity. Relaya even suggests a workspace name from your domain; you can change it.

## Joining an existing workspace

If your team already uses Relaya, you don't create a new workspace — you **accept an invitation** to the existing one. An admin invites your email, and you join automatically after signing in with the matching address. Invited teammates can use any email, including a personal one — see [Invite your team](/docs/using/team).

## Switching workspaces

If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them from your profile menu in the top-right. Your most recent choice is remembered for next time.
