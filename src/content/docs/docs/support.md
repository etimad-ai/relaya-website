We're a small team running a private pilot, which means you get direct, personal support.

## Contact us

- **General help and questions:** **support@relayahq.com**
- **Security issues:** **security@relayahq.com**
- **Privacy and data requests:** **privacy@relayahq.com**

During the pilot we answer support email directly, on a best-effort basis during business hours. When you write in, it helps to include your workspace name and, if something went wrong, what you expected to happen and what happened instead.

:::note
The pilot doesn't carry a formal uptime or response-time SLA. Your pilot agreement describes the support channel and expectations for your engagement.
:::

## Common questions

### How do I add my team?

An admin invites teammates by email from the Members page and assigns each a role. See [Invite your team](/docs/using/team).

### Can I sign in with a personal email?

To **create** a workspace you need a work email. To **join** an existing workspace by invitation, any email works — including a personal one. See [Sign in & your workspace](/docs/sign-in).

### Why isn't Relaya showing any deals or contacts yet?

Relaya builds from your connected communication, so it needs a mailbox connected and some history imported first. See [Connect email & calendar](/docs/connect-email-calendar).

### Does Relaya send emails on its own?

No. Agents can _draft_ actions like follow-up emails, but nothing is sent until a person approves it. See [Approvals](/docs/using/approvals).

### Is my data used to train AI models?

No — we don't use your data to build shared models, and content processed by our AI provider is handled under terms that prohibit training on it. See [Privacy](/docs/trust/privacy).

### How do I disconnect a mailbox or delete my data?

Disconnect any source yourself from Connectors. For exporting or deleting your whole workspace, email **privacy@relayahq.com**. See [Your data](/docs/trust/data).

### How do I stop using Relaya?

Contact **support@relayahq.com** to offboard. We'll disconnect your sources and delete your workspace data per your pilot agreement.
