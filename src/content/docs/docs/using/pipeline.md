title: Deals and pipeline
description: A board view of your deals with explainable health scores and suggested opportunities.
---

The pipeline is a board of your deals arranged by stage, from first contact to close. At the top, a summary line reads something like "N open deals · $X in play" so you always know where things stand.

## The board

Deals move left to right through six stages:

**Prospecting → Qualification → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Closing**

Each column header shows a stage dot, the number of deals in that stage, and their total value.

## Deal cards

Every card shows the deal name, its account, and its value. It also carries a **deal-health dot with a score from 0 to 100**, grouped into bands:

- **Healthy**
- **Moderate**
- **At-risk**
- **Critical**

The health score is always explainable and grounded in evidence — never an opaque number. Open a deal to see the factors behind it.

### Inside a deal

Opening a deal reveals its intelligence:

- **Health factors** — what's helping and what's hurting
- **Signals** — activity and buying signs Relaya has picked up
- **Recommended next actions**
- The **evidence** behind each of these

## Moving a deal

Use the **⋯** menu on a card to:

- **Move to another stage**
- **Mark won**
- **Mark lost**

:::note
Moving deals requires a write role. Viewers see the pipeline read-only. See [Invite your team](/docs/using/team) for roles.
:::

## Suggested deals

Relaya may surface **Suggested deals** — accounts showing buying signals but with no open opportunity yet. When one looks right, you can turn it into a deal in a single click and it drops into your board.

:::tip
Suggested deals are a good place to start your week. They point to interest you might otherwise miss.
:::
