Workspace **owners and admins** can invite teammates and control what each person can do through a **role**.

## Roles

There are four roles:

- **Admin** — full administrative access
- **Manager** — a write role for leading a team's work
- **Member** — a write role for everyday work
- **Viewer** — **read-only**

Write roles (Admin, Manager, Member) can edit records and approve actions. Viewers can see the workspace but cannot change records or approve anything in [Approvals](/docs/using/approvals).

## Inviting teammates

Invite someone by **email** and assign their role. You can:

- **Email the invitation** directly, and/or
- Share a **copy-able link**

The invitee joins after signing in with the **matching email address**. The role you chose applies as soon as they're in.

:::note
Creating a brand-new workspace requires a **work email**. But once a workspace exists, teammates on **any email — including personal addresses** — can join by invitation. Invite people wherever they're reachable.
:::

## Managing invitations

Pending invitations can be **revoked** at any time before they're accepted — useful if you sent one to the wrong address or someone no longer needs access.

:::tip
Assign the smallest role that lets someone do their job. Start people as Viewer or Member and raise their role if they need more.
:::
