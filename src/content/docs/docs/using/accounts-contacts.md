Accounts and contacts are the foundation of your CRM. **Accounts are companies. Contacts are people.**

## Accounts

The accounts list shows a card for each company with:

- A company **initial-avatar**
- **Industry**
- **Primary domain**
- A **lifecycle status** — for example _prospect_ or _active_

Open an account to view and edit its details.

## Contacts

The contacts list shows people, each with an avatar and the accounts they belong to. Open a contact to see:

- **Emails**
- **Phones**
- **Linked accounts**

## Creating and editing

You can create and edit both accounts and contacts yourself at any time. Fill in what you know — you don't need every field to get started.

## Let Relaya suggest them

Relaya can propose accounts and contacts from your [connected communications](/docs/connect-email-calendar). When it spots a person or company that isn't in your CRM yet, it suggests one.

:::note
Relaya proposes uncertain identities — it doesn't quietly create records from guesses. A person confirms what actually becomes an account or contact, so your data stays clean and trustworthy.
:::

## Importing in bulk

Already have your data in a spreadsheet? You can bring accounts and contacts in all at once. See [Import your data](/docs/using/import) for how CSV import works, including how re-importing avoids duplicates.
