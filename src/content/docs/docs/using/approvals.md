Relaya's agents can do real work — including drafting consequential actions like sending a follow-up email. But **a person must approve before anything leaves the system.** Approvals is where those pending actions wait for your decision.

## The core guarantee

This is Relaya's central governance promise:

> Agents draft. Humans decide. Nothing consequential happens unattended.

An agent can prepare an action, ground it in evidence, and line it up for you — but it cannot send it on its own. You stay in control of everything that reaches the outside world.

## Reviewing a pending action

Each pending action appears with a **full preview** so you can see exactly what would happen. For an email, that means:

- **To**
- **Subject**
- The full **body**

Nothing is hidden behind a summary — you review the real thing before it goes.

## Your decision

Two choices for each item:

- **Approve & send** — the action is carried out as previewed
- **Reject** — the action is discarded and nothing is sent

:::tip
Read the preview end to end. Once you approve, the action is sent exactly as shown.
:::

## Who can approve

Approving requires a **write role**. Viewers can see the approvals queue but cannot approve or reject — it's read-only for them. See [Invite your team](/docs/using/team) for how roles work.

## Grounded, not guessed

The actions you review here are grounded in the evidence Relaya has gathered — the same signals you can inspect on a [deal](/docs/using/pipeline). You always have the final say, and you can reject anything that doesn't look right.
