Today is your home screen in Relaya. Instead of asking you to hop between records, it gathers your most consequential work into one **focus queue**, ordered by what matters and when — not by record type.

## The stats strip

Across the top you'll see a quick read on your workspace:

- **Approvals** — actions waiting for your decision
- **Open tasks** — everything still to do
- **Meetings · 7d** — upcoming meetings in the next 7 days
- **Deals at risk** — turns amber when the number is above zero

These are your at-a-glance signals. Tap through to the matching area when a number needs attention.

## The focus queue

Below the strip is the queue itself. It blends four kinds of item together and orders them by consequence and timing, so the next thing to do rises to the top:

- Pending **approvals**
- **Tasks**
- Upcoming **meetings** (next 7 days, pulled from your connected calendars)
- **Deals at risk**

Each row shows a small glyph for its kind, a title, a one-line bit of context, and a timing or priority note — for example "Due tomorrow", "27/100 · Critical", or "In 2h".

### Quick actions

Every row has an action that fits its kind — **Open**, **Prepare**, **Inspect**, or **Review**. Tasks can also be marked **Done** right inline, without leaving Today.

:::tip
Work the queue top to bottom. If a row no longer needs you, clear it (mark a task done, review an approval) and the next priority takes its place.
:::

## Still setting up?

If your setup isn't finished, Today shows a **Continue setup** callout that points you to the remaining steps. Connecting your [email and calendar](/docs/connect-email-calendar) is what fills the queue with real meetings, tasks, and at-risk signals.

A **Data coverage** link takes you to the getting-started activation page, where you can see how much of your workspace is connected and flowing.
