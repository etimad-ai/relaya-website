title: How Relaya works
description: How Relaya turns your conversations into evidence-grounded memory and explainable deal health.
---

The heart of Relaya is the path from a raw message to something you can act on — kept faithful and explainable at every step. You don't have to think about this to use Relaya, but it helps to know what's happening under the hood.

## From conversations to memory

When you connect a mailbox or calendar, Relaya reads each message and meeting and links it to the right account and people (by email address and company domain). It then extracts **memory** — structured facts such as a commitment, an objection, a buying signal, a decision timeline, or a competitor mention.

Every fact Relaya remembers carries:

- **The evidence** — the exact quote, and the message it came from.
- **Whether it was said or inferred** — a fact stated directly is marked differently from a hypothesis Relaya drew.
- **A confidence level.**

Because each fact is tied to a real quote, you can see it, check it, and correct it — nothing is taken on faith.

## You stay in control of the record

Memory isn't a black box. You can **confirm**, **reject**, or **edit** anything Relaya remembers, and your edit takes precedence while the history is preserved. Where an identity is uncertain, Relaya proposes it and a person decides what becomes a record — it won't quietly invent accounts or contacts.

## Explainable deal health

Relaya turns memory and activity into **signals** — a competitor is present, a request went unanswered, a next step has stalled — and combines them into a **deal-health score** from 0 to 100.

The score is never just a number. Open a deal and you'll see the factors behind it, a plain-language explanation, and the evidence for each signal. It's a score you can defend in a forecast review. From there, Relaya suggests the **next best actions** and surfaces the deals most in need of attention in [Today](/docs/using/today).

## Why grounding matters

An AI CRM that invents context is worse than no CRM. Relaya's design goal is the opposite: if it can't cite the evidence, it doesn't claim the fact — and where evidence is thin, it says so rather than filling the gap.

Anything Relaya might _do_ on your behalf — like sending a follow-up — always waits for your approval. See [Approvals](/docs/using/approvals).
