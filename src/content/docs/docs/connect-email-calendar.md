Relaya's value comes from your real customer communication, so connecting a mailbox is the most important setup step. The moment a mailbox is connected, Relaya begins turning your messages and meetings into organized customer context.

## What you can connect

- **Gmail** and **Outlook** — your work email, for the messages that carry customer context.
- **Google Calendar** and **Outlook Calendar** — your meetings, which add participants and timing to that context.

## How to connect

1. Go to **Connectors** (or follow the setup steps on the getting-started screen).
2. Choose a provider and select **Connect**.
3. You'll be sent to that provider to sign in and grant access. Relaya only requests the permissions it needs to read your messages and calendar and to send on your behalf when you approve it.
4. After you approve, you return to Relaya and the source shows as connected.

:::note
You grant access through the provider's own consent screen. Relaya receives a secure authorization to your mailbox — never your password — and you can revoke it at any time (see below).
:::

## Importing history

A newly connected mailbox can bring in recent history so Relaya isn't starting cold. Choose a window — for example the last 30 or 90 days — and Relaya imports and processes those messages in the background. You can refresh a source later to pull in what's new.

## Managing connected sources

From **Connectors** you can:

- See the status of each connected mailbox and calendar.
- **Refresh** a source to import newer activity.
- **Disconnect** a source at any time. Disconnecting stops future syncing and revokes Relaya's access to that account.

## What Relaya does with it

Connected messages become **memory** — evidence-grounded context that powers your accounts, deals, and daily priorities. To understand that pipeline, see [How Relaya works](/docs/how-relaya-works). To understand how this data is protected, see [Security](/docs/trust/security) and [Privacy](/docs/trust/privacy).
